[ 04 ] WORKFLOW PROTOCOLS & FAQ OPERATIONAL DETAILS

Sessions are hosted over Frame.io for asynchronous timestamped feedback and annotations. For real-time grading, live NDI feeds or calibrated Louper streams provide sub-second latency directly to your desktop or iPad Pro with Rec.709 color fidelity.

Standard offline assembly is typically delivered within 3-4 working days from raw footage ingest. Full online conforms, final color passes, and sound mastering complete within 2-3 days following lock, totaling 6-7 business days for an uncompromised master.

All fixed commercial packages include three consolidated rounds of revisions prior to final master exports. Editorial structural changes requested after color grade lock or sound final conform are charged at the standard hourly cutting rate ($160/hr).

Deliverables required within a 48-hour window from footage ingest incur a 50% urgency premium on standard day rates. Weekend or public holiday suite operations carry a 35% surcharge and guarantee unbroken 14-hour machine availability.
